The Story of FloraLux Studio

From a small sunlit greenhouse in 2012 to a premier botanical atelier crafting unscripted floral art for living spaces.

Explore Our Journey Our Philosophy
Founding Journey

Our Story

FloraLux began in the spring of 2012 inside a repurposed Victorian glasshouse in San Francisco. What started as a passion project by founder Claire Vance—hand-wrapping wildflower stems for local neighborhood markets—gradually evolved into a recognized botanical studio.

Over the past decade, we have expanded our reach across metropolitan residences while remaining fiercely devoted to slow-flower cultivation, personal customer relationships, and organic arrangement practices.

Design Core

Our Floral Philosophy

We view floristry not as decorative assembly, but as living spatial sculpture built on three core tenets:

🌿 Natural Stem Curvature

We never force stems into rigid geometric wire shapes. We honor the natural bend, shadow, and movement of each living plant.

🎨 Tonal Color Gradients

Arrangements are built using painterly color transitions—blending soft blush into deep plum—rather than loud harsh contrasts.

💧 Zero-Foam Eco Mechanics

We reject toxic floral foam. Every arrangement relies on reusable ceramic pin-frogs, glass urns, and compostable twine binding.

Craftsmanship

Behind the Bouquet

Creating a signature FloraLux bouquet requires rigorous botanical conditioning and artistic balance:

  • 1. Dawn Selection: Stems are hand-picked at peak bud hydration.
  • 2. Underwater Precision Cut: Stems are recut under mineral spring water at 45° angles to prevent air embolisms.
  • 3. Hand-Tied Spiral Assembly: Florists layer foliage and focal blooms in a continuous spiral for balanced 360-degree viewing.
  • 4. Hydro-Gel Preservation: Base stems are encased in organic eco-gel packs for fresh transit.

Ethical Sourcing

From Growers to You

We believe great floristry starts with healthy soil. FloraLux partners directly with independent, eco-certified family farms across California and Oregon.

By eliminating middleman wholesale hubs, our stems arrive at our studio within 24 hours of harvest. We guarantee fair living wages for farm harvesters and strict zero-pesticide growing standards.
