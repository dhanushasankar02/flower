Peak Harvest Collection

Handpicked at First Bloom

Our seasonal curation highlights rare dahlias, ruffled garden ranunculus, and organic wild pods sourced from eco-certified heirloom farms. Every stem is harvested at dawn during tight bud stage, guaranteeing up to 14 days of vibrant bloom on your counter.

We honor the unscripted shape of natural flora, allowing stems to curve naturally with organic movement.

Aromatherapy Infusion

The Botanical Sensory Bar

Beyond visual beauty, memory is tied to aroma. At our exclusive Sensory Bar, master perfumers infuse organic cold-pressed essential extracts—including English lavender, Damask rose, and orange blossom—onto the bio-linen wrapping of your bouquet.

As the arrangement unboxes, subtle fragrance notes fill the room, deepening the emotional connection of your gift.
